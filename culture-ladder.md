*The Culture Ladder* by T. G. L. Hudson is due from CRC Press in February 2027. It reframes the safety culture ladder, asking what culture fits the risks an organisation runs rather than how "mature" it is.

The ladder originates with Dr P. T. W. Hudson, the author's father.

The book has its own website, which carries publication details and, once CRC Press issues them, ordering links.

Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) is the consulting and methods practice that applies the book's approach.
