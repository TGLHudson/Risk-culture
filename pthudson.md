Dr P. T. W. Hudson developed the safety culture ladder from Ron Westrum's three organisational cultures. He is the father of T. G. L. Hudson, and we state that relationship openly. His papers are listed here because the work of Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) builds on them. Papers he wrote with T. G. L. Hudson also appear on the main [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.

Each entry links to its Digital Object Identifier (DOI) where one exists. Papers published with the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) are available through [OnePetro](https://onepetro.org), the SPE's library.
