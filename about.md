Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) is the consulting and methods practice of T. G. L. Hudson.

## T. G. L. Hudson

T. G. L. Hudson works on operational risk, safety culture and barrier analysis. He has published some 25 conference papers and book chapters, most of them with the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE). His book *The Culture Ladder* is due from CRC Press in February 2027.

## Position

Safety is not the opposite of dangerous work. It is the condition of being able to repeat it. The work is pro-worker and pro-union. See [Approach]({{ '/approach/' | relative_url }}).

## Provenance

The safety culture ladder that this work builds on was developed by Professor P. T. W. Hudson, T. G. L. Hudson's father, from Ron Westrum's three organisational cultures.
