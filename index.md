<h1>Risk culture fitted to the risks you run</h1>

<p class="lead">Safety is not the opposite of dangerous work. It is the condition of being able to repeat it.</p>

Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) works on operational risk, safety culture and barrier analysis. The question we ask is not how mature an organisation's culture is, but whether it fits the risks the organisation actually runs.

We are not working for management; we are working against the risk. The aim of every engagement is that management and workforce agree on how the risks being run are managed.
