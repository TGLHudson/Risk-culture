## Working against the risk

The question we ask is not how mature an organisation's culture is, but whether it fits the risks the organisation actually runs.

We are not working for management; we are working against the risk. The aim of every engagement is that management and workforce agree on how the risks being run are managed. Trade unions and workforce representatives can contact us directly; see [Consulting]({{ '/consulting/' | relative_url }}).
