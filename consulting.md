Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) consults on operational risk, safety culture and barrier analysis.

## Who we work with

- **Operational, health, safety and environment leaders** in organisations that run serious hazards and need to know whether their culture fits those hazards.
- **Risk and governance functions** that are asked to assess, report on or change risk culture, and want a basis for doing so that is tied to the risks actually run.
- **Trade unions and workforce representatives**, who carry the risk on the shop floor and have an equal stake in how it is managed.

Whoever makes the enquiry, the workforce, and where present its union representatives, takes part in the work. The aim is agreement between management and workforce on how the risks are managed.

## For trade unions and workforce representatives

We are not working for management; we are working against the risk. That puts the people who are exposed to the hazard at the centre of the work, not at its edge. Unions and workforce representatives are welcome to contact us directly.
