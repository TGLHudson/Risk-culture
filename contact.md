Hudson Risk Culture (HRC) takes on consulting and licensing by enquiry, from organisations and from trade unions and workforce representatives alike.

## Enquiries

For consulting and licensing, email [enquiries@hudsonriskculture.com](mailto:enquiries@hudsonriskculture.com).

It helps to include:

- your organisation, or the union or workforce body you represent, and the sector;
- a brief, non-confidential description of the risks you are concerned with;
- whether you are asking about consulting, licensing a tool, or both.

<p class="placeholder">How enquiries are handled in confidence, and how a conflict is managed where Hudson Risk Culture is, or has been, engaged by the same employer.</p>

## General information

For anything else, including publications, speaking and press, email [info@hudsonriskculture.com](mailto:info@hudsonriskculture.com).
